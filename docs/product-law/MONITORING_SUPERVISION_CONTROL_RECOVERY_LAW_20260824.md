# SIMPROK Product Law — Monitoring, Supervision, Control, Reporting, SMKK, and Recovery

**Status:** OWNER-RATIFIED PRODUCT LAW<br>
**Date:** 2026-08-24<br>
**Scope:** Halaman 2 Monitoring / Project Control Room and its relationship to domain workspaces and Recovery<br>
**Authority:** Owner decision. Do not reinterpret as optional UX guidance.

## 1. Purpose

This document locks the product boundary for SIMPROK Monitoring so future AI agents, architects, and executors do not fragment Monitoring, Supervision, Control, Reporting, SMKK/Keselamatan, Cash Flow, Daily Reporting, and Recovery into competing truth systems.

The product principle is:

> **One governed project truth, many contextual workspaces and projections.**

SIMPROK remains **simple outside, rich inside**.

## 2. Page-2 shell remains stable

The approved Halaman 2 Monitoring shell remains the primary project-control workspace. Do not redesign it merely because deeper supervision/control capabilities are added.

The stable outer structure remains centered on:

- Project context;
- Active Baseline identity;
- RAB/WBS as the structural anchor;
- selected WORK_ITEM context;
- current/period context;
- contextual side workspace;
- progressive disclosure rather than permanent button forests.

New capabilities must normally grow **inside or behind this shell**, not replace the shell.

## 3. Canonical meanings

### Monitoring

Monitoring answers:

> **What is happening?**

It observes and presents governed project facts such as Actual, dates, quantities, weights, evidence, freshness, status, resources, incidents, and other current conditions.

Monitoring is not merely a report screen. It is the live project-control room.

### Supervision / Pengawasan

Supervision answers:

> **Is what is happening correct, compliant, acceptable, and sufficiently evidenced?**

It includes inspection, verification, quality conformity, quantity conformity, evidence/provenance, safety/SMKK conformity, method/compliance checks, findings, NCR-like findings, and authority-governed acceptance/verification.

### Control / Pengendalian

Control answers:

> **What is deviating, what is its impact, and what operational action is required?**

Control may include planned-vs-actual comparison, deviation, attention, corrective action, operational recovery action, resource adjustment, resequencing, productivity response, warnings, and other actions that remain within the currently valid contractual/baseline truth.

### Reporting / Pelaporan

Reporting answers:

> **How are governed facts, findings, deviations, decisions, and follow-up presented for a defined period and authority context?**

Reporting is a **projection of canonical facts**, not a second truth-entry system.

A weekly or monthly report must not independently invent progress, cost, safety, or quality numbers that disagree with canonical Monitoring facts.

## 4. Core law: report projection, not duplicate truth

The canonical law is:

```text
CANONICAL PROJECT FACTS
  + period/filter/context
  -> report projection
```

Not:

```text
Monitoring truth
+ separately typed weekly truth
+ separately typed monthly truth
```

Weekly and monthly reporting should evolve from the same canonical Monitoring table/facts through period selection and contextual projection.

## 5. Daily Report is special: intake/orchestration + projection

Laporan Harian is not merely a printable report and must not become a duplicate-entry silo.

It is allowed to act as a daily **capture/orchestration workspace** for governed field facts such as:

- work performed / Actual;
- workforce;
- equipment;
- material;
- weather;
- events/issues;
- quality facts;
- SMKK/safety facts;
- evidence/photos;
- notes.

Those facts must enter canonical domain records and then feed Monitoring and periodic reports.

Preferred law:

```text
DAILY FIELD FACTS
  -> canonical domain facts
  -> Monitoring
  -> daily / weekly / monthly projections
```

Do not make users re-enter the same fact into Monitoring, Daily Report, Weekly Report, and Monthly Report separately.

## 6. What Monitoring/Supervision/Control must eventually cover

Halaman 2 is expected to integrate or summarize, progressively and contextually, the following control dimensions without becoming a cluttered dashboard:

1. **Scope / Quantity / Volume** — RAB/WBS/BOQ, Actual quantity, accepted quantity, remaining work, change context.
2. **Time** — schedule baseline, planned-to-date, milestones, slippage, forecast, critical/near-critical context when lawfully available.
3. **Cost** — baseline/budget, commitment, actual cost, cash flow, payment/progress context, variance, forecast.
4. **Quality / Mutu** — quality plan, inspection, test, material approval, method/shop-drawing/ITP-like controls, findings, nonconformity, corrective action, acceptance.
5. **SMKK / Keselamatan Konstruksi** — safety-management facts and controls; not reduced to accident counts.
6. **Resources** — labor, equipment, material, productivity, delivery, availability, stock/consumption where relevant.
7. **Risk / Issue / Change / Decision** — risk, issue, instruction, RFI-like questions, variations/changes, decisions, claims/events, corrective actions.
8. **Evidence / Visual Intelligence** — photos, audiovisual, future fixed cameras, drone, satellite, sensors, and machine-derived observations through the same governed evidence backbone.

## 7. SMKK / K3 law

SIMPROK must not collapse SMKK into a simplistic accident-statistics widget.

For Government/Advanced product semantics, **SMKK / Keselamatan Konstruksi** is the broader domain. K3/worker occupational safety is part of that domain, not the whole domain.

The SMKK workspace may eventually cover, as applicable:

- RKK / safety planning;
- hazard/risk register;
- high-risk work analysis / AKK-like controls;
- permit to work;
- toolbox meeting;
- induction;
- PPE/APD;
- safety inspection;
- unsafe act / unsafe condition;
- near miss;
- incident / accident;
- corrective action;
- equipment safety;
- worker competency;
- public safety;
- environmental controls;
- traffic-management controls;
- emergency preparedness;
- open findings and overdue actions;
- daily/weekly/monthly safety reporting.

The locked front-door label must not be renamed casually. Future UX may use a clear user-facing label such as **Keselamatan (K3/SMKK)** or **SMKK / Keselamatan** only through a separate explicit product decision.

## 8. Cash Flow and other domain doors are not silos

Cash Flow, SMKK, Daily Report, Logistics, Quality, Schedule, Evidence, and similar domain workspaces are **specialized rooms connected to the same project truth**.

They must not create competing project identities, competing Actuals, competing baselines, or duplicate report facts.

Halaman 2 Monitoring may summarize their important current state and attention signals; users can open the domain workspace for detail.

## 9. Monitoring vs Recovery — canonical boundary

Owner decision is accepted with one durable refinement:

> **Monitoring governs execution against the currently valid official commitment. Recovery governs intervention design and formal-change pathways when normal control is insufficient or official truth may need to change.**

The boundary is **not merely “no addendum vs addendum.”**

A serious Recovery Plan may exist without an addendum. Conversely, any action that changes official contractual/baseline truth must not be silently performed by Monitoring.

### Monitoring owns

Monitoring may:

- observe and compare current facts;
- detect deviation;
- perform operational control within the current contract/baseline;
- manage ordinary corrective actions;
- track resource additions, shift changes, productivity actions, resequencing, and similar measures when they remain lawful within the current official commitment;
- report current condition and attention;
- recommend escalation to Recovery.

### Recovery owns

Recovery is the dedicated workspace for:

- recovery/problem definition when normal control is insufficient;
- scenario simulation and option comparison;
- intervention design;
- recovery planning;
- proposed changes to official time, scope, value, specification, design, or other controlled commitments;
- change governance;
- re-baseline proposal;
- addendum process when required;
- authority review/approval and effective-date governance.

## 10. Baseline law

### Monitoring must never rewrite the Baseline to hide deviation

Bad:

```text
Baseline target 55%
Actual 43%
-> silently change Baseline to 43%
```

Correct:

```text
Baseline target 55%
Actual 43%
Deviation -12%
```

### Recovery also does not directly rewrite official truth

Recovery first creates scenarios/proposals. They remain proposal/simulation until proper authority formalizes them.

Only after approved governance (including addendum where required) may a new versioned Baseline become ACTIVE.

The previous Baseline remains historical and auditable; it is not deleted.

## 11. Original commitment vs revised commitment must remain visible

Recovery must not erase history.

SIMPROK must be able to explain, for example:

```text
Original Baseline finish: 30 Nov
Deviation occurred: -15%
Recovery approved: 12 Oct
Revised Baseline finish: 20 Dec
Current status vs revised Baseline: On Track
```

Therefore "On Track" against a revised Baseline must never imply the project was never previously late.

## 12. Closed control loop

The intended product loop is:

```text
OBSERVE / MONITOR
  -> SUPERVISE / VERIFY
  -> COMPARE WITH OFFICIAL BASELINE
  -> CONTROL WITHIN CURRENT COMMITMENT
  -> if normal control is insufficient: RECOVERY
  -> scenario / governance / formal change if required
  -> new official version when approved
  -> MONITOR AGAIN
```

Reporting follows the entire journey and remains auditable.

## 13. Progressive-disclosure UI law

Halaman 2 must remain simple outside.

It may progressively surface, only when data and law support them:

- Kondisi Terkini;
- Pengawasan / verification attention;
- Deviation;
- Attention / risk;
- operational control actions;
- Recovery escalation.

Do not show false or premature intelligence. If schedule truth does not exist, do not fabricate schedule deviation. If evidence is unverified, do not present it as verified.

## 14. Integrity law applies to every domain

Permanent SIMPROK law:

> **INPUT != TRUTH**

Therefore:

- evidence received != evidence verified;
- authorized submit != semantically true;
- capture method != trust classification;
- machine observation != official fact;
- stored for audit != eligible for official calculation;
- suspected invalid != proven false.

Future phone/camera/drone/satellite/sensor/machine inputs must join the same governed evidence and Actual backbone. Do not build parallel truth stores for each device/channel.

## 15. Official Actual Calculation Eligibility is a hard next-stage gate

Before official earned progress, deviation, S-Curve, forecast, or recovery intelligence consumes Actual, SIMPROK must explicitly decide which lifecycle states are eligible.

Do not silently assume that SUBMITTED, VERIFIED, or ACCEPTED is the correct calculation authority.

Status:

> **OFFICIAL_ACTUAL_CALCULATION_ELIGIBILITY = UNDECIDED / NEXT-STAGE PRODUCT GATE**

This decision must precede official deviation/S-Curve/control math.

## 16. Agent implementation law

All AI agents/executors working on Monitoring, Supervision, Control, Reporting, Daily Report, SMKK, Cash Flow, Schedule, Quality, Evidence, or Recovery must follow:

1. Read this law before designing or modifying relevant architecture.
2. Reuse the approved Halaman 2 shell; do not redesign it without concrete contrary evidence and Owner approval.
3. One canonical fact may have multiple projections; do not duplicate truth stores.
4. Monitoring controls execution against the current official commitment.
5. Recovery designs/escalates interventions and governs formal truth changes.
6. Monitoring cannot silently re-baseline.
7. Recovery proposals are not official until governed approval/effective date.
8. Reports are projections, not competing truth inputs.
9. Daily Report may orchestrate intake, but canonical domain records remain authoritative.
10. SMKK is broader than a K3 accident counter.
11. Evidence/device channels do not bypass integrity/authority/lifecycle law.
12. Preserve original vs revised commitments for audit.
13. If healthy: PASS -> LOCK -> ADVANCE.
14. If broken: prove exact defect -> repair minimum seam -> retest.
15. No speculative rebuild, no parallel truth, no silo architecture.

## 17. Product summary

**Halaman 2 Monitoring** is the unified project-control room.

**RAB/WBS** is the stable structural anchor.

**Monitoring** observes current governed reality.

**Pengawasan** checks conformity, evidence, quality, quantity, safety, and governed acceptance.

**Pengendalian** manages deviation and operational corrective action within the current official commitment.

**Pelaporan** projects canonical facts into daily/weekly/monthly/final views without inventing separate truth.

**Laporan Harian** is a daily field-fact orchestration/capture surface plus report projection, not a duplicate-entry silo.

**SMKK / Keselamatan** is a substantive control domain, broader than a simple K3 incident counter.

**Cash Flow and other doors** are specialized workspaces connected to the same truth backbone.

**Recovery** is the intervention/scenario/change-governance workspace when normal control is insufficient or official commitments may need to change, including addendum/re-baseline pathways when required.

**One project. One governed truth. Versioned official commitments. Rich intelligence inside. Simple experience outside.**
